Case Study

ECNL Scheduling Engine

A scheduling system built to automate tournament and league scheduling, reduce manual effort, and generate balanced game schedules using custom scheduling logic.

Python Google Automation

The Challenge

The project presented two significant challenges:

First, creating balanced schedules for ECNL competitions required accounting for team availability, team ranking, travel considerations, league rules, and numerous scheduling constraints that were traditionally managed through a time-consuming manual process. Building schedules manually often required multiple revisions and significant coordination.

Second, at the time, I had only minimal Python experience from the iXperience Data Analytics Bootcamp and had never built a software application—let alone an automated scheduling system. Although, the timeline was only seven months, I was confident that with extensive research, persistence, and guidance from professors, the development of a solution that automated much of the scheduling process would be attainable.

The Solution

The ECNL Scheduling Engine automated much of the scheduling process by organizing league constraints into a repeatable workflow. The application generated schedules more efficiently while allowing users to review, adjust, and validate results before finalizing the schedule. Automation reduced repetitive work while improving scheduling consistency.

Impact & Outcomes

The Scheduling Engine significantly reduced the manual effort required to create balanced competition schedules by automating repetitive scheduling tasks and organizing complex constraints into a structured workflow. As the project evolved, I incorporated an interactive dashboard that gave the organization real-time insights into each tournament. Users could quickly view annual schedules, compare match difficulty based on team rankings, identify the highest- and lowest-ranked teams attending, and analyze how many teams from each region were participating.

What began as a scheduling tool ultimately became a decision-support platform that streamlined planning while providing valuable competitive insights. In the future, I would like to make the UI more appealing to make it feel more professional and sporty. A more custom feel to make the client feel as if this is made for them only. Also, anytime I catch myself inputting or basically dragging my mouse to click something in the process, I try to figure out how in this system can it be automated.
