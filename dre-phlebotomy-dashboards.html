Case Study

DRE & Phlebotomy Dashboards

Two public-facing geospatial dashboards that help users locate Drug Recognition Experts and phlebotomists by Missouri county through interactive maps and contact information.

Power BI ArcGIS Geospatial Analytics Public Information

The Challenge

Contact information for Drug Recognition Experts and trained phlebotomists needed to be easier for law-enforcement partners and the public to locate. A traditional list or spreadsheet made it difficult to understand geographic coverage, identify the closest available resource, and quickly access the appropriate county-level contact information.

Live DRE Dashboard

Explore Drug Recognition Expert coverage and contact information by Missouri county.

View on the Missouri Coalition for Roadway Safety website ↗

The Solution

The solution combined interactive Power BI reporting with county-based geospatial visualization. Users can hover over or select a Missouri county, review the available local contacts, and move between the statewide map and detailed information. Separate dashboards were created for Drug Recognition Experts and phlebotomy resources while maintaining a consistent, easy-to-use experience.

Live Phlebotomy Dashboard

Explore county-level phlebotomy resources and contact information across Missouri.

Impact & Outcomes

The dashboards made statewide resource information easier to explore and more practical to use. Instead of searching through static lists, users can quickly identify county-level coverage and access the relevant contact information through a visual, public-facing interface. The result supports faster coordination and clearer access to impaired-driving enforcement resources across Missouri.
