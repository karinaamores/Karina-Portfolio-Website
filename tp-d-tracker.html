Case Study

TP-D Tracker

A centralized tracking tool designed to organize Transportation Planning Division progress, improve workflow visibility, and support clearer project coordination and reporting.

Power BI Everviz Oracle

The Challenge

Transportation Planning Division assignments and project updates were being tracked across separate spreadsheets, emails, and individual notes. This made it tedious for each driver to update their progress. Some were quarterly, bi annually, or seasonal. This made it even more inconsistant and harder to see up-to-date numbers Many times it was even harder to cross-train a new employee. Documentation was scarse.

Most of the progress charts were made through Everviz. Useful, when you don't have another option, but it is definietly not user-friendly and at times can be finicky once published. Other charts were made in word docs. Again, sometimes data points would not align and labels would be covered. Updating these was also very hard as the connection between excel and word would sometimes break.

The Solution

The TP-D Tracker brought progress details, ownership, and status updates into one structured reporting tool. The solution was designed to make information easier to enter, filter, review, and communicate while giving the team a clearer view of active work and upcoming priorities. With some of these measures, a live database connection could be made. It turned those measures into a live progress measure.

Impact & Outcomes

The tracker created a more consistent and visible workflow for the division. Team members could more quickly identify assignment owners, deadlines, project status, data entry, and areas needing follow-up. The centralized structure reduced time spent searching through separate files and supported clearer status updates, workload awareness, and coordination across the team.
